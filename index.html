01 / RESEARCH

From signals to understanding.

My Ph.D. at IIT Guwahati explored automated diagnosis of heart valve diseases from phonocardiogram signals using deep learning. Today, I bring that signal-first perspective to industrial intelligence and autonomous systems.

01

Signals & healthcare

Time-frequency analysis of non-stationary signals, cardiovascular signal processing, and PCG, ECG, and SCG analysis.

02

Applied machine learning

Deep learning for detection and classification, laser weld quality monitoring, and process control automation.

03

Autonomous systems

Agentic AI, retrieval augmented generation, and the design and development of embedded systems.

Projects & workshops

Automated heart valve disease detection

Ph.D. thesis, 2017–2023. A framework combining novel phonocardiogram features and machine learning to detect heart valve abnormalities. Mentor: Prof. S. Dandapat, IIT Guwahati.

Underwater acoustic pinger system

M.Tech thesis, 2015–2017. Design, development, and experimental validation of an underwater communication system for source detection and localization. Mentor: Dr. SRK Vadali, CSIR-CMERI, Durgapur.
